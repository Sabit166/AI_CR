Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 15 October 2026

If you used the Messenger chatbot

AI CR does not store your messages, your name or your Facebook ID, so we hold no personal data about you to delete. Your conversation with our Page is stored by Facebook. To remove it:

  1. Open Messenger and find the conversation with our Page.
  2. Open the conversation settings and choose Delete chat.
  3. To also remove the app's access, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find AI CR and click Remove.

If you are a class representative (CR)

Your CR account holds your email address and the exams you posted. To delete them, send a message to our Facebook Page on Messenger with the email address of your account and the words "delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply. You can choose whether the exams you posted are removed too or kept for your classmates.

See also our Privacy Policy.
