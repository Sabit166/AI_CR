Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

AI CR is a student project that helps students find exam dates, syllabus and notes posted by their class representatives (CRs). It has two parts: a Discord bot in each class's channel and the exam list website. This policy explains what data each part uses.

The Discord bot

When you use one of the bot's slash commands, Discord sends our server the command, the channel and server it was used in, your Discord user ID and your roles and permissions in that channel. We use them to:

  • find the class that channel belongs to and look up its exams in our database, which holds only exam information posted by CRs;
  • check whether you are a CR before giving you the editor link;

We do not save your questions or your name. When a CR saves an exam, we store the Discord user ID of the CR who saved it. Our server logs record which command was used and how long it took, never what was asked or who asked. Discord keeps the messages in your server under Discord's Privacy Policy. Questions are answered by our own server; they are not sent to any AI service.

The bot also posts exam updates in the class channel (every Friday, the evening before an exam day, and when a CR asks it to). For each class we store its name, its Discord server, channel and CR role IDs, and a record of which automatic updates were posted.

The exam list website

Each class's exam list opens from a link the bot shares in that class's channel; there are no accounts. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. CRs open the editor from a private link the bot sends them; the link expires after a few hours and is kept only in that browser tab. Our hosting providers (Vercel and Render) may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and request time, for security.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone other than the service providers named above, which we need to run the bot and the website.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about students. A CR who wants the Discord user ID removed from the exams they saved, or a class that wants all its data deleted, can ask the project maintainer through their CR or server admin. Removing the bot from a server stops it from receiving anything from that server; within a few minutes that server's classes are switched off (their links stop working). Their exam data is kept until deletion is requested, so adding the bot back restores them.
