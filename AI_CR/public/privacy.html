Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

AI CR is a student project that helps students find exam dates, syllabus and notes posted by their class representatives (CRs). It has two parts: a Messenger chatbot on our Facebook Page and the exam board website. This policy explains what data each part uses.

The Messenger chatbot

When you send a message to our Facebook Page, Facebook forwards it to our server together with your Page-scoped ID (a number Facebook assigns to you for this Page only). We use them to:

  • work out what you are asking. The text of your message is sent to OpenAI's API, which classifies it as a question about a course, date or exam type;
  • look up matching exams in our database, which holds only exam information posted by CRs;
  • send the answer back to you through Facebook Messenger.

We do not save your messages, your name or your Facebook ID. The ID is held only in memory while we reply. Our server logs record that a message was answered and how long it took, never what it said or who sent it. Facebook keeps its own copy of the conversation under Meta's Privacy Policy, and OpenAI handles API requests under its own policy, which does not use API data for training.

The exam board website

Students can browse the exam board without an account. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting providers (Vercel and Render) may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and request time, for security.

CRs sign in with an email address and password, managed by Supabase. We store the CR's email address and the exam details they post. The sign-in session is kept in the CR's browser.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone other than the service providers named above, which we need to run the bot and the website.
